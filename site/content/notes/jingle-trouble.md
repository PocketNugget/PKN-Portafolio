# Jingle Trouble

Hack The Box University CTF 2025 was called Jingle Trouble. Hackztecs finished 52 of 1,014 teams. That put UDLAP in the top 5%, and it was the only Mexican university in the top 100. It was my first top-100 finish at a University CTF.

I sat it with Georgina Zerón, Natalia María Caballero Garza, Daniel Martínez Maimone, Juan Francisco Cruz Baquedano, and Juan Pablo Morales Durante. Juan Pablo had been president before me. He is the reason I started taking this work seriously.

The board was web, network, and forensics. Burp Suite and Ghidra stayed open. I am not posting the solves. The scoreboard is the public part.
